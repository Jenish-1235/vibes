# Context document — REPLACE EVERYTHING BELOW WITH YOUR OWN

This file is the agent's entire brain. Everything it writes — replies, posts,
DMs — is grounded in what you put here. The agent refuses to run while this is
a stub. Be detailed; more context = better drafts. A good doc covers:

## What we're building
One or two paragraphs. What it is, what stage it's at, what's true today vs
planned. The agent will not claim anything beyond what you write here.

## The problem
The pain we're solving, in the words your customers would use.

## Who we want to talk to (ICP)
Roles, company types, the situations they're in. This drives relevance scoring
during `scan` — be specific about who is and ISN'T a fit.

## What we're trying to learn (discovery questions)
The 3-5 things you'd ask on a discovery call. Replies and DMs steer toward
these.

## The ask (CTA)
Exactly what we want from a conversation, e.g. "a 15-minute call to hear how
they handle X today". If you have a booking link and want it used in DMs, say
so explicitly — otherwise no links go out.

## Voice
How you write on Twitter. Paste 3-5 of your own real tweets here as examples —
this matters more than adjectives.

## Hard rules
Anything the agent must never say or do (competitors not to trash, claims not
to make, topics to avoid).
